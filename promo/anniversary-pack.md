## 1. Long form (website / press / proposal)

**MINDFIRE HOMES: 10 YEARS OF REDEFINING MODERN LIVING**

Ten years ago, we started with a simple question: why should owning a genuine, quality home in Abuja be so
complicated?

That question became Mindfire Homes.

The risk in buying property here is rarely the price. It is the title: whether the land is what the seller
says it is, whether the documents survive scrutiny, and whether the infrastructure in the brochure has been
built. We built this business around answering those questions **before** a buyer is asked for money.

For ten years, that has meant five things on every property:

1. **We check the title first.** Title type and status are confirmed with the land registry before a property is listed. If a search raises something unresolved, the listing does not go up.
2. **We put the documents in your hands.** Search results, survey plan, deed of assignment and payment schedule, shared in full, not summarised.
3. **We only list where infrastructure exists.** Road access, power and water delivered, not promised.
4. **We take you to the site.** Accompanied visits, including mid-construction, so you see the build and the finishes yourself.
5. **We put the terms in writing before you commit.** Deposit, milestones, duration, every cost. Nothing appears later.

In a decade, we have:
- Helped 1,000+ proud property owners secure their homes and land, since 2016
- Sold homes in communities like Project Elysian at Nibeco Gardens
- Offered flexible payment plans, agreed in writing before commitment

Our services, from land to keys:
1. **Estate development**
2. **Land banking & sales**
3. **Building & construction**
4. **Property investment advisory**
5. **Documentation & title perfection**
6. **Facility management & after-sales support**

To every client, partner and team member who trusted us: thank you.

We are not just selling property. We are redefining modern living, one checked title at a time.

The next ten starts now. **Secure assets, not excuses.**

*Mindfire Homes · redefining modern living · mindfirehomes.com*

## 4. Upgrade path: make the big numbers true *and* provable

The draft wanted "1000+" because big numbers sell. They do, so earn the right to use yours this week:

1. **Count.** Pull every signed sale agreement / allocation letter since founding. That number is your headline.
2. **Name.** List every estate or development you have sold in, with location. Named projects beat round numbers.
3. **Show.** Ask 5 happy clients for a 20-second phone video holding their documents ("I got my C of O"). Real faces outsell any statistic.
4. **Publish.** Put the count, the projects and the testimonials on the About page (`MILESTONES` and `TEAM` in `app/about/page.tsx`). The film and flyer pick them up on the next render.
